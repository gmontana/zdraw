# Support

- Bugs and numbers that do not hold: open an issue with the
  `bug_report` or `measurement_report` template (attach `zdraw doctor --json`
  and, for timing, a `zdraw bench` card line).
- Benchmark cards from your Mac: the `benchmark_card` issue template, or a
  pull request appending your card to `community/results.jsonl`.
- Questions and ideas: GitHub Discussions.
- Security: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (private advisory).
- Commercial use: see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md). For paid support or
  integration enquiries, open an issue without confidential details.
